01 / THE PLAYGROUND

A studio for
the wonderfully
unexpected.

A shape becomes a rhythm. A rhythm becomes a sculpture. The next idea is yours to discover.

Start with frames, orbits, blooms, ribbons, and more. Change their materials and movement, bring pieces together, or reshape a surface with your own hands.

Step inside the studio ↗
01 / FIND A FORM

Begin anywhere.

Explore ten editable forms, from nested frames to ribbons and spirals. Every starting point is yours to change.

02 / SET IT IN MOTION

Give it a rhythm.

Choose how pieces move, then tune their speed, spacing and amplitude until the composition feels right.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Shape the unexpected.

Pull, twist and inflate the surfaces. Save your favorite creations and download a moving artwork loop for free.
