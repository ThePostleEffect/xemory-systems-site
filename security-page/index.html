A few practical details.

How much does it cost?

Services start at $99. Pricing is determined case by case based on the systems involved and the agreed work. Your quote is confirmed before setup begins.

Can you work remotely or come to me?

Both options are available depending on the work. We'll confirm whether remote setup is suitable, or discuss location coverage and any travel arrangements before booking.

Who receives and responds to alerts?

We'll agree on alert destinations and explain the next steps during setup. Continuous monitoring and ongoing support are not included unless separately agreed.

Will this stop every attack?

No security setup can guarantee that. These measures add layers to your existing protection and can help detect selected suspicious activity. Regular updates and good account practices still matter.
